# AI Document Builder Brief Template

Use this template when briefing Gamma or another AI document / slide builder.

Paste a fully filled version into the tool. Do not rely on linked files.

## Role

Build a customer-facing incident follow-up document for a mixed audience of:

- customer executive stakeholder
- customer technical lead
- internal leadership if needed

This document is:

- an executive incident memo
- a fuller follow-up after an earlier summary
- a decision-support and trust-repair document

This document is not:

- a sales deck
- a marketing asset
- a forensic appendix

## Positioning

An earlier incident summary has already been sent.

This new document must be presented as:

- `Updated following the [DATE] review call`
- or `Fuller post-incident review and action plan`

It should not read like a contradiction of the earlier note.

## Tone

- calm
- current
- credible
- accountable
- operationally useful
- not defensive
- not sales-led

## Brand and design rules

- Use the current company branding if available.
- If exact brand assets are unavailable, use restrained navy / slate styling rather than generic purple cyber themes.
- Cover page may be dark, but body pages should default to light backgrounds.
- Light pages with a few dark accent cards are acceptable if readability remains strong.
- Keep metadata and confidentiality text high-contrast and easy to read.
- Keep footer and page treatment consistent.
- Avoid decorative cyber imagery.

## Hard layout constraints

- Maximum 120 words of narrative text on any one card, excluding tables.
- Maximum 5 bullets in any one column.
- Do not use dense 3-column layouts for long text.
- If a page feels crowded, split or simplify it rather than shrinking text.
- Keep body text large and comfortable to read.
- Keep the action plan in one table with an explicit owner column.
- Expand acronyms on first reference for mixed or executive readers.

## Card structure

Card 1:
- Cover page
- Title
- Subtitle
- Optional short status line

Card 2:
- Executive summary
- Immediate threat
- Investigation status
- Residual risk

Card 3:
- What changed since the earlier summary

Card 4:
- What we know now
- Confirmed by us
- Reported by customer
- Still being confirmed

Card 5:
- Consolidated timeline
- Use only the most decision-relevant events

Card 6:
- What limited earlier visibility
- Plain-English gap headings

Card 7:
- What we are changing now
- Clear status labels

Card 8:
- Joint action plan
- Action
- Owner
- Target timing
- Status
- Purpose

## Final self-check

Before finalising, check:

- Can a non-technical executive understand each card in 20 seconds?
- Does any page feel crowded?
- Does the report feel like a working memo rather than a deck?
- Are our own gaps stated plainly?
- Is the action plan unmistakably clear?
